FAQ

What replays are available here?

Only publicly available replays from Pokemon Showdown. Absolutely NO private replays are used here.

How often does this get updated?

It should automatically update every 12 hours. Exceptions may occur for maintenance.

Can you show replays from over a month ago?

No. Due to the sheer volume of replays, anything over a month would max out the RAM memory usage. It would also make searching much slower.
